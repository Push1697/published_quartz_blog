#### Q. What are licenses in Google workspace ?
**Ans**: A license is a utility which a user in the Google workspace tenant needs to access the services present in that suite. such as gmail, Google Drive, Calendar etc. 

**A user needs a license to use a Google service**. For example, a user must have a license for Google Workspace before they can use Gmail, Google Drive, or any other Google Workspace tool. A user can have a license for more than one service.

### Types of licenses
---
**There are two types of licenses:**

1. *site-based license* 
2. *user-based license.*

### Site-bases license
---
When we sign up for a service that has a site-based license, such as Android, every user assigned the license automatically in that organization.

### User-based licenses
---
such as a Google Workspace license may need to be assigned depending on your subscriptions, and this can be done manually or automatically.

# Assign user licenses

In some cases, Google automatically assigns a license for a service to every user in your entire organization. In other cases, you can manually assign licenses to your entire organization, an organizational unit, a group of users, or an individual user. You can also turn on the option to **automatically assign licenses to users**.

we can assign and manage licenses in three places in the Google Admin console:

- **The Billing page**: Useful for assigning licenses to all users in your organization or an uploaded list of users, or to set up automatic licensing.
- **The Users page**: Useful for assigning licenses to multiple users, but not all users in your organization. You can manage licenses for multiple users using a CSV file.    
- **An individual user’s page**: Good for managing a single user.
---
### Turn off automatic licensing

**Step 1**
If you are not already signed in, sign in to your domain as the Administrator at [admin.google.com](http://admin.google.com/) 
**Step 2**
From the main menu, navigate to **Billing**, then select **Subscriptions**.

**Step 3**
From the list of subscriptions, select **Google Workspace Business Plus**.

**Step 4**
In the **License settings** section, click **Manage licensing settings**.
 
**Step 5**
Click on the **Automatic licensing** setting and select **OFF** from the menu.

**Step 6**
Click **SAVE**.

# Notes about automatic licensing
- If you have multiple Google services or multiple subscriptions of the same service, **you can only turn on automatic licensing for one service or subscription**.
- If you choose to automatically assign licenses for a service to all users, **you can’t remove an individual user’s license**.
- If you have organizational units under your top-level organization, **you can set automatic licensing options for specific organizational units**.
- It can take up to **24 hours for an automatically assigned license to take effect**.
- If turning on automatic licensing for a subscription results in assigning more than the available number of licenses, some users might not get a license.
