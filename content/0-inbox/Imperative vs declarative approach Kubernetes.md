# Imperative approach
In this approach we give the instruction to achieve something. i.e. step by step what I need to do to reach the destination.



such as - To make tea 
- boil water
- add tea leaves
- add 2 spoon sugar
- add milk
- and again boil the whole content for 10 minutes
- tea is ready

Above approach is called Imperative approach.
