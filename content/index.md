## Three ways in

**Reading a series.** Long-form material is written in order, and every part
states where it sits and what comes next. Start at part 1 and the garden reads
like a course.

**Looking something up.** Reference notes, runbooks and cheat sheets stand on
their own. Use the search box, the topic tags, or the sidebar grouped by section.

**Fixing something right now.** Troubleshooting notes come from real incidents
and lead with the symptom, so you can match what you are seeing before reading
the explanation.
