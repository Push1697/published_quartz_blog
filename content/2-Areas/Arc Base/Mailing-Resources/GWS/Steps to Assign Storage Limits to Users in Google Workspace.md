1. **Log in to the Admin Console**  
   Log in to [admin.google.com](https://admin.google.com) using your admin account.

2. **Navigate to Organizational Units**  
   Go to **Directory** > **Organizational Units** (as shown in the image).
![[Pasted image 20250122133407.png]]
1. **Create a New Organizational Unit (OU)**  
   After selecting **Organizational Units**, click **Create a new OU**.
![[Pasted image 20250122133503.png]]
2. **Move Users to the New OU**  
   Once the OU is created, move the users to whom you want to apply storage limits. You can follow this guide to [move users to an OU](https://support.google.com/a/answer/182449?hl=en).

3. **Set Storage Limits**  
   After moving the users to the OU, go to the **Storage** section in the menu bar (as shown in the image), then click **Manage**.
![[Pasted image 20250122133525.png]]
![[Pasted image 20250122133558.png]]
4. **Select the Created OU**  
   Choose the created OU from the list.
![[Pasted image 20250122133607.png]]
5. **Set User Storage Limits**  
   Under the **User Storage Limit** card, fill in the required storage amount (in GB) and save the settings.
![[Pasted image 20250122133616.png]]
