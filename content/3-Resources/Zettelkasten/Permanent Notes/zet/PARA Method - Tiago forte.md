## Project

Project is an goal or actions that have certain outcome or something that I want to achieve. - Tiago forte video note on 30 seconds.

It is because the project main aim is to focus on your work.

Mera project ek defined and realistic approach ke sath hona chahiye. jiska koi outcome ho, jise mein achieve krna chahta hun.

#### Project components
It should have 2 main key things.
1. Have goals
2. Have an end  [ should have deadline ]

e.g.
1. Finishing an article and publish it is an project
2. Going for a vacation this year is an project.

##### Step 1 :
- Top of mind projects
- check your email for any reminders or something
- Check your calendar
- Check your to do app or task list
##### Step 2 : Follow the 10 To 15 Rule
- Keep withing 10 - 15 projects only.
- Make significant and visible progress
- Never get stuck on a project
##### Step 3 : IDENTIFY FALSE PROJECTS
- Dreams
- Hobbies
- Mega Projects
