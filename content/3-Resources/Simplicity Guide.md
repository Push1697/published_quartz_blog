# 🧘 The Simplicity Guide

## Core Principles

### 1. Capture First, Sort Later

Don't worry about where a note goes when you first have the idea.

- **Use Terminal**: `qn "Idea..."`
- **Use Inbox**: Everything lands in `0-inbox`.
- **Trust the Process**: You will sort it during your daily/weekly review.

### 2. Linking Over Filing

Folders (PARA) are just buckets to hold things. The real magic is links.

- **Don't** stress about "Is this Project or Area?". Pick one.
- **Do** link related notes together. `[[Note A]]` is related to `[[Note B]]`.

### 3. Atomic Notes

- One idea per note.
- If a note gets too long, break it into smaller notes.
- Use the `Templates/New Zettel` for standard formatting.

### 4. The Home Dashboard

Start your day at `[[Home]]`.

- Check your **Inbox**.
- Review **Active Projects**.
- Jump to **Areas** you want to focus on.

> " Complexity is the enemy of execution."
